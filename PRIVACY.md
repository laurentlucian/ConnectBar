# Privacy

ConnectBar has no analytics, telemetry, accounts, advertising, or hosted service.

It runs the open-source `asc` CLI on your Mac to request data from Apple. ConnectBar never reads or stores your App Store Connect private key. Cached signals, selected apps, and preferences remain in your macOS user defaults.

If notifications are enabled, their text may contain app names, release states, and review excerpts. macOS controls how those notifications appear.
